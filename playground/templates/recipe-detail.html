{{recipe.title}}

A hearty, nourishing bowl packed with seasonal roasted vegetables, fluffy quinoa, and a vibrant herb dressing that brings everything together.

check_circle In Your Kitchen

  • eco 1 cup Quinoa
  • eco 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • eco Salt & Pepper

shopping_cart Missing Ingredients

  • 2 large Sweet Potatoes
  • 1 bunch Fresh Parsley
  • 1 Lemon

Calories 420

Carbs 54g
Fats 18g

Instructions

1

Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Wash and cube the sweet potatoes into 1-inch pieces.

2

Toss the sweet potatoes with 1 tbsp of olive oil, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 25-30 minutes until tender and golden at the edges.

3

Rinse the quinoa thoroughly. In a medium pot, combine quinoa with 2 cups of water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes.

4

Blend the fresh parsley, remaining olive oil, lemon juice, and a pinch of salt in a food processor until smooth to create the herb dressing.

5

Assemble the bowls by dividing the cooked quinoa, topping with roasted sweet potatoes, and drizzling generously with the herb dressing.
